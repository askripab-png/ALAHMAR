The Perfect Tournament: Zero Goals Conceded

Hosted at Sultan Qaboos Sports Complex in front of a thunderous home crowd, Oman staged one of the most dominant defensive tournaments in Gulf football history. Legendary goalkeeper Ali Al-Habsi kept 5 clean sheets in 5 matches.

Final Match: Oman 1 - 0 Saudi Arabia
Golden Goalscorer: Hassan Rabia (60')
Best Goalkeeper: Ali Al-Habsi (0 conceded)
Kuwait 2017/18

23rd Arabian Gulf Cup

خليجي 23 الكويت - دراما ركلات الترجيح

🏆

Heroics at Jaber Al-Ahmad International Stadium

Under Dutch tactician Pim Verbeek, Al-Ahmar overcame early group adversities, eliminated Bahrain in the semi-finals, and defeated the United Arab Emirates in an electrifying penalty shootout that sent Muscat into nationwide jubilation.

Final Match: Oman 0 - 0 UAE (5-4 PKs)
Hero of the Night: Faiz Al-Rushaidi (2 PK Saves)
Tournament MVP: Ahmed Kano (Captain)
